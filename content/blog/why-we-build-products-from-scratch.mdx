Many digital agencies sell **templates**. Fast, cheap, and the result looks
just like everyone else's. But once your business grows, templates become a
bottleneck: inflexible, hard to maintain, and often slow.

At Akusara Digital, we take a different path: **build from scratch**. Why?

## 1. Built for your business, not assumptions

Templates are made for everyone. Products are made for **your business**. Every
workflow, data field, and payment integration we design matches how your
business operates — not the other way around.

## 2. Maintainable long-term

Products we build have **code we know**. When there's a bug, we know where to
look. When you want a new feature, we know the structure. Third-party
templates? There's always "tension" every time you change something.

## 3. Performance is a priority, not a bonus

Templates are often heavy because they accommodate every possibility. Custom
products only carry what you **actually use** — lighter, faster, better for SEO.

## 4. No vendor lock-in

The code is yours. If you ever want to switch teams or bring it in-house,
everything is documented and clean. No "lock-in" hassles.

---

**The bottom line:** templates are for quick MVPs. Products are for growing
businesses. We choose the latter — because that's what takes your business to
the next level, not just online.
